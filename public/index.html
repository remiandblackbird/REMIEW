Module 02 — NIP Calculator

Turn ingredient specs into a draft Nutrition Information Panel.

Enter each ingredient's quantity in the recipe and its nutrient values from its spec/PIF — or upload the spec and let AI pull the numbers out for you.

⚠ This produces a calculated estimate based on the ingredient data you provide. It is not a substitute for FSANZ's Nutrition Panel Calculator or lab analysis — and if the finished product will carry a nutrient content claim (e.g. "low fat", "high protein", "source of fibre"), those thresholds should be confirmed against verified data, not a calculated estimate alone.
